Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Fathomdrift mobile application (“Fathomdrift,” “the App,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the App on Apple iOS devices.

1. Who we are

Fathomdrift is an offline deep-dive meditation app. Core features — browsing journeys, playing bundled water-pressure and bubble audio, logging sessions, and reading guides — work without creating an account and without requiring an internet connection.

2. Summary

  • We do not require you to register or sign in.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not embed advertising or third-party analytics SDKs in the App.
  • Session history and optional notes stay on your device.

3. Information stored on your device

Depending on how you use Fathomdrift, the App may store locally:

  • Dive session records (journey name, duration, reached depth, timestamps)
  • Optional private notes you attach to a session
  • Preference toggles such as haptics and welcome state

This data remains in the App’s sandbox on your iPhone unless you delete the App or clear the log.

4. Internet and audio

Meditation audio ships inside the App bundle. Playback does not download sound files at run time and does not show a network loading progress bar for core listening. Opening this Privacy Policy reads a local HTML resource.

5. Permissions

Fathomdrift uses the device audio session for local playback. It does not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, precise location, or tracking for personalized ads.

6. Children

The App is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

8. Contact

For privacy questions about Fathomdrift, contact the developer using the support details listed on the App’s product page in the Apple App Store.
